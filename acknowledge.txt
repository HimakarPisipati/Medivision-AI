## Datasets
- **COVID-19 Radiography Database**: The deep learning model in this project was trained using publicly available, anonymized academic datasets containing chest X-rays for COVID-19, Viral Pneumonia, and Normal cases (e.g., datasets curated by researchers from Qatar University, the University of Dhaka, and collaborators, commonly hosted on platforms like Kaggle).

- **EfficientNetB0**: The pre-trained convolutional neural network architecture utilized for transfer learning in this project was originally developed by researchers at Google Brain.
